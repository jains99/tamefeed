# TameFeed privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

TameFeed is a browser extension that changes how x.com and twitter.com look in your browser. It is not affiliated with X Corp.

## What TameFeed stores
TameFeed keeps two things in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`):

- **Your settings**, including your mute list and the accounts you allow media from.
- **Seen post ids**: the ids of up to 5,000 posts you scrolled past, used to dim or hide posts you have already seen.

## What TameFeed does not do
- It does not send any data anywhere. The extension makes no network requests.
- It does not collect, sell or share personal information.
- It does not use analytics, ads or tracking.
- It does not load or run remote code.

TameFeed reads the content of x.com and twitter.com pages only to hide, blur or restyle it on your device.

## Permissions
- `storage`: saves the settings and seen post ids described above.
- Access to `x.com` and `twitter.com`: needed to restyle and filter those pages. TameFeed runs on no other site.

## Removing your data
Uninstalling TameFeed deletes everything it stored.
